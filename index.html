Journal Entry Automation — Configured Once
These replace manually built JEs. Click any to see how it works.
Today (Manual)

Finance team opens the prior month's JE, changes the date, re-enters the amount (sometimes hunting down the invoice first), re-codes the GL, and posts. Repeated for every lease at every park — every single month.

NetSuite Template

A Memorized Transaction is set up once in NetSuite per lease. It contains the vendor, GL debit/credit coding, amount, and recurrence schedule. On the 1st of every month, NetSuite creates a draft JE automatically. Finance reviews the list, spot-checks amounts against the invoice, and approves with one click. No typing. No hunting. No re-coding.

Finance Team's New Role

Reviewer and approver. Your job is to verify the amounts are correct — not to create the entry. If a lease changes, you update the template once. The system handles the rest forever.

Today (Manual)

Check arrives from sponsor. Finance team creates a JE, manually determines how much to recognize now vs. defer, codes it, posts it — and then remembers to come back monthly to recognize the next tranche. It's a spreadsheet and a prayer.

NetSuite Revenue Recognition

When a sponsorship contract is loaded into NetSuite, the Revenue Recognition schedule is set once. NetSuite automatically creates monthly recognition entries for the contract period. When the check arrives, it's matched to the open deferred revenue balance automatically via bank feed matching. Finance sees a clean dashboard of "recognized vs. deferred" by sponsor.

Finance Team's New Role

Contract input (once per sponsor, per season) and monthly revenue review. You're now analyzing whether sponsorship revenue is tracking to forecast — not manually splitting checks between accounts.

Today (Manual)

Monthly check from Best Lockers or G&H Vending arrives. Finance team creates a JE from scratch: pick the vendor, pick the GL account, enter the amount, post. Sometimes the check gets lost in a park GM's inbox first and finance finds out a week late.

NetSuite + Bank Feed

NetSuite's bank feed integration monitors the bank account. When a check or ACH from Best Lockers clears, it's automatically matched to an expected receivable that's already set up for each vendor at each park. Finance sees a "matched" notification rather than creating the entry from scratch. Unmatched items surface for human review — the exceptions, not the routine.

Finance Team's New Role

Exceptions-only review. You're looking at the 3 items that didn't auto-match (vendor paid late, amount changed, wrong park), not processing the 47 that came in clean. That's a morning task, not a full day.

Today (Manual)

Pass revenue sits in a spreadsheet until it's "earned." Finance manually moves amounts from deferred liability to earned revenue each month, or after the season, or whenever someone remembers. Auditors ask questions. It gets messy.

RocketRez + NetSuite

RocketRez recognizes season pass revenue at the time of sale and provides GL-level reporting to support your accounting workflow. Sales data is available via API to support your NetSuite integration — giving finance the detail they need without manual extraction.

Finance Team's New Role

Configuring the recognition rule once and running variance analysis — comparing deferred revenue balance to prior seasons, flagging unusually high or low recognition rates by park, and feeding that into the forecast model. That's strategic finance work.

Each integration domain has a clear owner, a defined pattern, and an explicit responsibility boundary. RocketRez owns the entitlement layer; partners connect through clean APIs.

PMS / Opera Cloud
Owner: RocketRez + Oracle OHIP
Bidirectional: RocketRez issues tickets and confirms entitlements from reservations. Room-key validation at gate. Folio charges post back to Opera via OHIP API. Real-time for gate validation; scheduled for folio settlement.
Finance / NetSuite
Owner: RocketRez (sub-ledger → JE)
RocketRez categorizes revenue at point of sale and generates structured journal entries by GL code, property, and revenue type. Automated delivery to NetSuite on a defined schedule — no manual intervention.
Guest Identity / Vantage
Owner: Vantage (wallet + loyalty) ↔ RocketRez (entitlements)
Vantage reads entitlement status from RocketRez to surface pass details in-app. Wallet transactions and loyalty events sync back. Guest identity is established at purchase and maintained through the visit lifecycle.
F&B / Toast
Owner: Toast (POS) — optional integration
Toast handles F&B ordering and kitchen operations independently. Integration to Opera Cloud for room-charge posting and to RocketRez for entitlement-based meal-plan validation where applicable.
Access Control / Gate Hardware
Owner: RocketRez
Real-time entitlement validation at every scan point via RocketRez API. Offline fallback caching ensures gates keep moving if connectivity drops. Validation resumes automatically on reconnect.
Spa & Activities / Booker
Owner: Property-level — Booker / Anemone
Reservation systems for spa and activities at applicable properties. Integration scope includes guest identification for entitlement validation and folio posting where activity charges apply to the PMS record.
