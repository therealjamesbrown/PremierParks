Executive Positioning and Delivery Plan

More than ticketing: your strategic transformation partner for Premier Parks

We bring combined decades of attractions experience and will partner with Premier Parks to deliver its vision: move from non-standard, independently managed operations to a repeatable, unified portfolio operating model. Ticketing and entitlements remain the control plane, but our role is broader: design, orchestrate, and support the full ecosystem across guest experience, operations, and financial governance.

Strategic Partnership

A combined team with deep attractions experience focused on unified guest experience, entitlement control, and portfolio standardization.

The Vision

One seamless guest experience across every park — simplified from arrival to checkout, with every property running on the same platform.

Our Role

We are not only a ticketing vendor. We are the transformation partner that owns entitlement control while orchestrating integration across PMS, F&B, identity, and finance.

Recommended Architecture

Vantage + RocketRez + Toast — cashless wallet, unified guest identity, entitlement control, and best-in-class F&B. Stronger digital experience and better long-term fit for Premier Parks' guest-facing ambitions.

Positioning Statement
We are not only a ticketing vendor. We are the platform partner that enables Premier Parks to run a connected ecosystem. Our responsibility is to own the entitlement backbone end-to-end and create clean interfaces that allow PMS, F&B, identity, and financial systems to plug in without compromising control.
Executive Summary Why Us Why Now Why This Plan
Board-Level Case We own the hardest and most operationally critical layer: ticketing, entitlement logic, and access validation across all channels. The timeline leaves no room for architectural drift; ownership must be explicit before build scale begins. What Fails Without a Ticketing Core
Key risk statement for the RFP
PROGRAM RISKS
  • No canonical entitlement source creates disputes at gates, POS, and partner systems
  • Inconsistent pass benefit enforcement causes revenue leakage and guest dissatisfaction
  • Disconnected systems multiply reconciliation, exception handling, and manual support
  • Financial cutover risk increases when operational events are not normalized at source
What RocketRez Owns in Ticketing
  • Product catalog governance — dated, any-day, season passes, groups, birthdays, cabanas, and add-ons
  • Entitlement engine — tiered benefits, sister-park access, capacity controls, and offer eligibility
  • Real-time issuance and validation APIs across web, mobile, gate, and POS channels
  • Operational controls for refunds, exchanges, voids, and full auditability
  • Cross-border support for Quebec taxes, language, and compliance requirements

    NetSuite Journal Entry Templates — Built Once, Run Forever
    These replace the JEs your team is currently building manually every month. Click any to expand how it works in NetSuite.
    Today (Manual)

    Finance team opens the prior month's JE, changes the date, re-enters the amount (sometimes hunting down the invoice first), re-codes the GL, and posts. Repeated for every lease at every park — every single month.

    NetSuite Template

    A Memorized Transaction is set up once in NetSuite per lease. It contains the vendor, GL debit/credit coding, amount, and recurrence schedule. On the 1st of every month, NetSuite creates a draft JE automatically. Finance reviews the list, spot-checks amounts against the invoice, and approves with one click. No typing. No hunting. No re-coding.

    Finance Team's New Role

    Reviewer and approver. Your job is to verify the amounts are correct — not to create the entry. If a lease changes, you update the template once. The system handles the rest forever.

    Today (Manual)

    Check arrives from sponsor. Finance team creates a JE, manually determines how much to recognize now vs. defer, codes it, posts it — and then remembers to come back monthly to recognize the next tranche. It's a spreadsheet and a prayer.

    NetSuite Revenue Recognition

    When a sponsorship contract is loaded into NetSuite, the Revenue Recognition schedule is set once. NetSuite automatically creates monthly recognition entries for the contract period. When the check arrives, it's matched to the open deferred revenue balance automatically via bank feed matching. Finance sees a clean dashboard of "recognized vs. deferred" by sponsor.

    Finance Team's New Role

    Contract input (once per sponsor, per season) and monthly revenue review. You're now analyzing whether sponsorship revenue is tracking to forecast — not manually splitting checks between accounts.

    Today (Manual)

    Monthly check from Best Lockers or G&H Vending arrives. Finance team creates a JE from scratch: pick the vendor, pick the GL account, enter the amount, post. Sometimes the check gets lost in a park GM's inbox first and finance finds out a week late.

    NetSuite + Bank Feed

    NetSuite's bank feed integration monitors the bank account. When a check or ACH from Best Lockers clears, it's automatically matched to an expected receivable that's already set up for each vendor at each park. Finance sees a "matched" notification rather than creating the entry from scratch. Unmatched items surface for human review — the exceptions, not the routine.

    Finance Team's New Role

    Exceptions-only review. You're looking at the 3 items that didn't auto-match (vendor paid late, amount changed, wrong park), not processing the 47 that came in clean. That's a morning task, not a full day.

    Today (Manual)

    Pass revenue sits in a spreadsheet until it's "earned." Finance manually moves amounts from deferred liability to earned revenue each month, or after the season, or whenever someone remembers. Auditors ask questions. It gets messy.

    RocketRez + NetSuite

    RocketRez flags season pass revenue as deferred at point of sale and pushes it to a deferred revenue GL code in NetSuite. NetSuite recognizes it on the redemption date — or spreads it across the season automatically based on the recognition rule set at configuration. No spreadsheet required. The liability balance updates in real time.

    Finance Team's New Role

    Configuring the recognition rule once and running variance analysis — comparing deferred revenue balance to prior seasons, flagging unusually high or low recognition rates by park, and feeding that into the forecast model. That's strategic finance work.
